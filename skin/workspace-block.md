## RNZ brand skin

The RNZ brand skin (rnz-fabric-apps) is applied to: {{APPS}}.

When you add or change UI in those folders, follow the "RNZ skin" block in that folder's
`AGENTS.md` and its `.agents/skills/rnz-skin/SKILL.md`: use the app's tokens or the RNZ variables
(`var(--rnz-red)`, `var(--rnz-grey)`, `var(--rnz-light)`, `var(--rnz-border)`), no new hardcoded
colours, Frutiger then Arial with no font links, weights 300, 400 and 700, light theme, the RICOH
logo only as the lock-up, and brand fixes change values only, never structure. This block is managed
by the RNZ skin tool.
