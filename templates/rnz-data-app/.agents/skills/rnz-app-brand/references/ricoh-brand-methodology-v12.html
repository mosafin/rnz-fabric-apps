How to use this document

This is the RNZ standard for how things look and behave on screen. It is written for designers, developers and AI builders. Copy, SEO and campaign messaging live in their own skills and are only referenced here.

Use it for

  • Colour, type, spacing, shape and elevation
  • Logo, tagline and Imagination Balloon use on screen
  • Components and their states
  • App shells and AI interfaces ("skins")
  • Design tokens for code
  • Rules for AI-generated pages and UI

Look elsewhere for

  • Website and email copy: rnz-brand-writer
  • SEO, metadata and schema: rnz-seo-geo-specialist
  • LinkedIn and social: rnz-linkedin-writer
  • Sitecore classes: rnz-design-elements-guide.html
  • Tables: RNZ Tables & Structured Data Design Standard.html
  • Fabric Apps build kit: rnz-fabric-apps template (RNZ Data App), Section 13

Five rules if you read nothing else

  1. Red, white and grey make up 80–90% of any screen.
  2. Ricoh Red is for the one primary action and key emphasis.
  3. Text is Ricoh Grey. Never black, never near-black.
  4. No eyebrow labels. Headings stand on their own.
  5. Use master artwork for the logo and Imagination Balloon. Never draw them. The logo is always the lock-up with its tagline.

02

Brand foundations

What the brand stands for decides how the interface should feel. These are the global definitions, not local reinterpretations.

Mission and vision

Ricoh empowers individuals to find Fulfillment through Work by understanding and transforming how people work, so their potential and creativity can realise a sustainable future.

How Ricoh describes itself

Use the exact phrase "workplace services provider" when saying what Ricoh is. The global focus areas are process automation, workplace experience and IT services.

Creative principles

Three principles govern tone of voice and tone and manner across all external communication (Guidelines §4). They apply to interfaces as much as to print.

PrincipleRoleIn designIn wordsIn an interface
CloseFundamentalBright colours with soft tones. Care shows in the details. Close-ups of people that convey emotion without sentimentality.Genuine and warm. Easy to understand, open, honest. No jargon.Plain labels, helpful empty states and errors, generous white space, real people in imagery.
ActiveTransformationalNew angles, freeze-frame images, scalable graphic elements.Innovative and lively. Direct, active verbs, the reader's point of view.One clear next step per screen, verb-led buttons, responsive feedback on every action.
UnlimitedAspirationalSimple, imaginative layouts. Imagery that speaks of possibility.Simple and imaginative. Confident, never boastful.Uncluttered screens, restrained colour, no hype or superlatives in UI copy.

How RNZ digital should feel

Professional, approachable, practical, human, outcome-focused. When in doubt:

  • Clarity over decoration. Every element earns its place.
  • Content first. If the layout draws more attention than the message, simplify.
  • One primary action per page or screen.
  • White space is a feature, not empty room to fill.
  • Consistency builds trust. One Ricoh experience across site, apps and documents.
03

Logo and tagline

Only master artwork is used. Never retype, redraw, recolour or rebuild the logo or tagline in HTML, CSS or SVG.

Lock-up logo: always

The RICOH logo with the "imagine. change." tagline logo below it. RNZ always uses the lock-up, on websites, apps, documents and AI-built interfaces. Minimum on-screen size is 80 px wide. Protected area is 0.6X on all sides, where X is the height of the "H". Master file: RICOH-Logo_sRGB_full-colour.png (never renamed).

Never the logo without the tagline

RNZ does not use the RICOH logo on its own, at any size. Where the lock-up can't be shown at 80 px wide or more (favicons, very small UI), show no logo. Size app headers to fit the lock-up: 40 px tall gives about 92 px wide. Never crop the tagline off the lock-up to make a logo-only version. For apps and portals this departs from global §3.3.9 and needs APAC brand approval (Section 16).

Colour

Ricoh Red and grey on white. Use the solid white version on Ricoh Red, dark imagery or other strong colour. Never place it on a background with too little contrast.

Never

  • Change the colour, proportion, angle or font. Never add effects, outlines or shadows.
  • Animate, move or deform the logo, including in video and app splash screens. Only the Imagination Balloon may be animated.
  • Place anything inside the protected area, or combine the logo with other shapes to make a new mark or app icon.
  • Put the logo or tagline on the Imagination Balloon.
  • Use the tagline logo on its own. In running text, write the tagline exactly as imagine. change. (lower case, full stops).

Apps and app icons: The guidelines list Software, Hardware and Service Portal as separate application categories (§3.3.9). A new app icon, launcher icon or co-branded mark is a new use of the brand. Get it approved through the APAC brand team before release.

Partner logos: On RNZ-made material, the RICOH lock-up sits on the right. Both logos keep their protected areas and appear at equal visual weight. A dividing line, if used, should not be in either company's corporate colour (§3.3.6).

Not part of the RNZ palette

  • Black and near-black: #000000, #111111, #1A1A1A, #333333. No dark backgrounds, dark bands, dark heroes, dark table headers, dark footers or dark tooltips.
  • Invented tints and shades (e.g. pillar "pastels", #B01025, #A50F22). The only functional shade is #A51022 for pressed red.
  • Legacy or generic reds: #CC0000, #990000, #FF0000.
  • Framework defaults: Bootstrap blue #007BFF, #DC3545, Material purple.
  • Gradients on brand colours.

Colour usage rule

Red, white and grey should make up roughly 80–90% of any screen. Secondary colours are for information graphics and small accents. Area of Expertise colours appear only in their own pillar context. If a screen looks like a rainbow, remove colour until it doesn't.

Two reds, one rule: The global guidelines define Ricoh Red as Pantone 186C, sRGB 207/18/46 (#CF122E). The RNZ Colour Chart and ricoh.co.nz use #CF142B. The difference is not visible on screen. RNZ digital work uses #CF142B. Use #CF122E only when matching supplied global artwork. Never mix the two in one product.

Pastel values: The global guidelines' colour page (§3.7.3) and conversion chart (§3.7.4) list slightly different pastel values. The RNZ Colour Chart uses the conversion-chart hex values shown above. Keep to those.

What this means in practice

  • Text is Ricoh Grey on white or #F5F5F5, or white on Ricoh Red, Ricoh Grey or a pillar colour. No other text pairings.
  • Input borders use Ricoh Grey (5.74:1). A light #E0E0E0 or #CCCCCC border fails the 3:1 non-text contrast requirement (WCAG 1.4.11).
  • Colour is never the only signal. Status, errors, chart series and pillars always carry an icon, text label or pattern as well.
  • Secondary colours in charts need direct labels or a 2 px white separator between marks, because none reach 3:1 on white.
  • Focus is always visible: a 2 px Ricoh Red ring with a 2 px white gap.
  • Targets are at least 44 × 44 px on touch screens (WCAG 2.2 minimum is 24 × 24).
  • Structure is sequential: one H1, then H2 and H3 with no skipped levels. Every input has a visible label. Every meaningful image has alt text, and decorative images use alt="".
  • Lists styled without bullets keep role="list", because Safari and VoiceOver drop list semantics when list-style: none is set.
  • Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Typography

Frutiger is Ricoh's specified typeface. Where Frutiger cannot be used, Arial is the substitute (Guidelines §3.6). No other typeface is used in RNZ production work.

Frutiger Light · 300

Large intro text, hero sub-lines, big numbers. Not for small text.

Frutiger Regular · 400

Body copy, form values, table data.

Frutiger Medium · 500

Sub-headings and UI labels that need slightly more presence.

Frutiger Bold · 700

Headings, buttons, key figures. Arial has no Light or Medium, so those weights fall back to Regular.

Font stack and loading

font-family: 'Frutiger', 'Frutiger LT Std', Arial, sans-serif;
  • Web (Sitecore): ricoh.co.nz provides the licensed font. Exported HTML and CSS declare the stack only. They never link a font service such as Google Fonts or a CDN.
  • Web apps and other hosts: Frutiger is a licensed Monotype/Linotype webfont. It is not available through Adobe Fonts. Self-host it only under a web licence that covers the domain. Without a licence, the stack falls back to Arial, which is correct.
  • Native apps: Embed Frutiger only under an app licence. Otherwise use Arial. Android does not ship Arial, so bundle a licensed Arial or accept the system sans-serif as a last resort.
  • Documents (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, PDF): Use Frutiger if installed, otherwise Arial.
  • Prototype fonts (Inter, DM Sans, Outfit, Roboto, Helvetica) must not reach production.

Rules

  • All text is Ricoh Grey. Hierarchy comes from size and weight, not darker colours.
  • Emphasis in red: one or two key words in a headline may be Ricoh Red. Only one highlight per heading, and never on body text.
  • Sentence case everywhere: headings, buttons, labels, tabs and table headers. No all-caps labels and no letter-spaced kicker text.
  • No eyebrows. Small labels above headings are not part of the Ricoh system. Headings carry the message. Category or date metadata sits below a title as plain small text.
  • Line length 60–75 characters for reading text. Left-aligned, never justified.
  • Bold only for emphasis, never whole paragraphs.

The Imagination Balloon

The main graphic element of Ricoh communication. It presents copy and imagery in a direct, friendly way. It is a rounded square with a small square tail at one corner. It is not a circle or a teardrop.

Assets: rnz-context/Imagination Balloon/ (Ribbon RNZ fill.png, Ribbon RNZ White Fill.png, Ribbon Red.png). Translucency comes from opacity on the white master, never from a CSS colour filter.

Do

  • Use master artwork only: PNG or SVG from the brand team or the RNZ asset folder.
  • Give it a bold, confident presence. It anchors a layout and is never a tiny ornament.
  • Use Version A (tail bottom-right) first. Use Version B only when the layout needs it.
  • Colour it Ricoh Red, grey or translucent white (about 70%). For non-corporate uses, secondary colours are allowed.
  • Keep text inside the permitted area, with at least 0.06X clearance from the edges.
  • Animate it gently where motion is used. It is the only brand element that may move.

Never

  • Draw it in CSS or SVG (e.g. border-radius: 50% 50% 50% 0). That creates a different shape.
  • Tint the PNG with CSS filters to pillar colours, or colour it in a pastel.
  • Stretch, rotate, flip upside down, crop or use only part of it.
  • Outline a coloured balloon, or combine it with other shapes.
  • Put the RICOH logo or the tagline on it, or use it in place of the logo.
  • Use it as a UI component: chat bubbles, buttons, avatar frames, loaders or map pins.
  • Imitate it with decorative circles in heroes or CTA bands.

Check before wider use: The RNZ outline ("Ribbon") balloons in pillar colours are a local variant. The global guidelines only allow outlines on white balloons over white or grey imagery (§3.5.9). Confirm APAC approval before using them in new apps or campaigns. Until then, the filled Red and White masters are the safe default.

08

Imagery and icons

Imagery should feel credible, local and practical. If an image could belong to any IT vendor, it is not Ricoh enough.

Use

  • Real people in real NZ workplaces: offices, schools, councils, clinics, production floors
  • Technology in use: devices, screens and MFPs in context
  • Natural collaboration, hybrid meetings, natural light
  • Close-ups that show emotion without sentimentality (Close)
  • Fresh angles and freeze-frame moments (Active)
  • Open, simple compositions that suggest possibility (Unlimited)

Avoid

  • Handshakes, boardroom stare-downs, people smiling at the camera
  • Hooded hackers, matrix code, red warning screens, "war room" cyber imagery
  • Holograms, globes, floating interfaces, abstract blue tech backgrounds
  • Obviously AI-generated or uncanny people
  • Dark photos used as a way to get a dark background

Image treatment

  • Don't put text over a busy image. Use a white content card on the image (Hero C) or place text beside it.
  • If a scrim is unavoidable, use Ricoh Grey at 60% (rgba(102,102,102,.6)) and check contrast. Never use a black gradient.
  • Crop to the subject. Keep aspect ratios consistent within a set: 16:9 heroes, 4:3 or 1:1 cards.
  • Use the RNZ image sizes in rnz-context/RNZ Image Ratios/ for Sitecore banners and thumbnails.
  • AI-generated images follow the same standard and must never show real, identifiable people, customer logos or Ricoh devices that don't exist.

Icons

  • Source: RNZ_Icon_Library.html in rnz-context. Use one set per product, with consistent stroke weight.
  • Colour: Ricoh Grey by default. Ricoh Red for the primary or active icon. A pillar colour only on that pillar's pages.
  • Sizes: 16 or 20 px inline, 24 px UI, 40 px feature, 56 px hero support.
  • No emoji as UI icons, no 3D or cartoon icons, and no mixed icon packs.
  • Icon-only buttons have an accessible name (aria-label) and a tooltip.

Elevation

Shadows are tinted with Ricoh Grey (Cool Gray 10C), never black. Use --shadow-sm for cards, --shadow for raised panels and --shadow-lg for menus, toasts and modals. Most surfaces need a border rather than a shadow.

Grid and breakpoints

  • Max content width is 1160 px. Gutters are 16 px on mobile and 32 px on desktop.
  • Breakpoints: 600, 768, 1024 and 1280 px. Design mobile-first, with no horizontal scrolling at 320 px.
  • Cards sit in 1, 2, 3 or 4 columns. An incomplete last row aligns left.

Accent bars

  • Top accent: 4 px on a card, in red or the pillar colour.
  • Left accent: 3–6 px on callouts, quotes, hero panels and the active nav item.
  • Heading rule: a 48 × 3 px red bar, optional, used under a section heading.
  • Use one accent style per component. Never stack a top and a left accent.

Dark mode

RNZ products ship in the light theme only. Black and near-black surfaces are not part of the RNZ palette, so no black or charcoal dark theme is built. If a platform requires a reduced-glare mode, propose a Ricoh Grey (#666666) surface with white text (5.74:1) to the brand team first. Grey is a Ricoh primary colour and needs no black.

AI assistants and chat

  • Messages: the user's message is on #F5F5F5. The assistant's message is white with a 3 px red left accent. Both have a 1 px border and 8 px radius.
  • Never shape messages, avatars or the assistant icon like the Imagination Balloon. The balloon cannot be stretched to fit text.
  • Naming: call the assistant "Ricoh assistant" or a plain descriptive name. Don't give it a persona, mascot or invented character without brand approval.
  • Voice: Close, Active and Unlimited apply. Plain NZ English, active verbs, short answers, no hype, and say what is unknown.
  • Actions: confirm before acting. Offer one primary red action and a neutral alternative.
  • Transparency: label AI-generated content and summaries. Show sources where the answer uses customer or company data.

Motion

  • Keep motion short and purposeful: 150–250 ms, ease-out. Use it to show cause and effect, not decoration.
  • The RICOH logo and lock-up never animate. The Imagination Balloon may move gently.
  • Honour prefers-reduced-motion. No flashing (WCAG 2.3.1), parallax or auto-playing carousels.

Rules for AI builders

Paste this block into any AI tool that generates RNZ pages, app screens, documents or skins. It is intentionally short so it survives context limits.

RNZ BRAND RULES (Ricoh New Zealand) — follow exactly.
COLOUR: Ricoh Red #CF142B (primary action, links, focus, key emphasis). Ricoh Grey #666666 (ALL text and icons). White #FFFFFF and #F5F5F5 (all backgrounds). #E0E0E0 (borders only). Pressed red #A51022.
Red + white + grey = 80–90% of the screen. Secondary colours (#FF9AA8 #FFC13D #96C120 #00A9C5 #5EA8E6) only in charts/illustrations. Pillar colours (#606CBF #517D32 #0F7AC9 #00328F) only as thin accents on that pillar's pages.
NEVER: black or near-black (#000, #111, #1A1A1A, #333) as text or fill; dark sections, dark heroes, dark footers, dark mode; gradients; Bootstrap/Material default colours; pastel fills behind body text.
TYPE: font-family 'Frutiger','Frutiger LT Std',Arial,sans-serif. Headings bold, sentence case. Body 16px/1.6. No external font links. NO eyebrow/kicker labels. No ALL CAPS labels or buttons.
SHAPE: radius 4px buttons/inputs, 8px cards, 12px heroes/modals. Accent bars: 4px top or 3–6px left, red (or pillar colour on pillar pages).
BUTTONS: one red primary per view; secondary = white with red border; neutral = white with grey border. Verb-led labels, min 44px high.
LOGO + BALLOON: use supplied master files only. The RICOH logo is always the lock-up with the "imagine. change." tagline, at least 80 px wide; never the logo alone, never a logo favicon; where it can't fit, show no logo. Never rename the master files. Never draw, recolour, animate, crop or combine the RICOH logo or the Imagination Balloon (a rounded square with a small square tail, not a circle). Never use the balloon as a chat bubble or UI shape.
IMAGERY: real people in real NZ workplaces; no handshakes, hooded hackers, holograms, globes or AI-looking people.
ACCESSIBILITY: WCAG 2.2 AA. Grey text only on white/#F5F5F5; white text only on red/grey/pillar colours. Visible labels, visible focus, alt text, one H1, colour never the only signal.
VOICE: NZ English, plain, warm, direct, active verbs, no hype or superlatives. Ricoh is a "workplace services provider". Tagline written exactly: imagine. change.
WHEN UNSURE: choose the simpler layout, white background, one red action.

Self-check before returning output

  1. Search the output for #000, #111, #1a1a1a, #333, black, text-transform: uppercase and letter-spacing on small labels. Remove every one.
  2. Confirm there is exactly one primary red button per view and one H1 per page.
  3. Confirm there are no external font or CSS links, and no CSS-drawn balloons or logos.
  4. Confirm every text and background pairing is one of the passing pairs in Section 05.
